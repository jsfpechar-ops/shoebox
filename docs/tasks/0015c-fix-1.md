## 1. Objective

0015c stopped at step 2: with a Dialog or Sheet open, axe reports `aria-hidden-focus` on two `<span>`s. They are Radix focus guards (`data-radix-focus-guard`), which Radix inserts around a modal so Tab wraps inside it. They are invisible, never hold focus for longer than one redirect, and are the documented Radix pattern; the dialog content itself passes. The orchestrator accepts excluding them from the axe context. This is the only change to the brief's test file, and the orchestrator owns it.
